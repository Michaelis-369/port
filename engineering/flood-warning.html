Community infrastructure · Rwanda

Flood Early Warning System

A sensor-to-alert pipeline and diversion channel designed for Ndera, Kigali — a community exposed to seasonal flooding that destroys crops and property with little warning.

Sole designer 2024 Ndera, Kigali, Rwanda
30+
Schools reached at Kigali Science Day 2024
The Constraint

No existing warning infrastructure, and minutes matter.

Ndera floods seasonally with little advance notice to farmers. Any warning system needed to be low-cost, low-maintenance, and reliable enough to trigger real evacuation and crop-protection decisions — not just a sensor reading nobody trusts.

The Decision

Filter noisy readings before they ever reach a threshold check.

Raw water-level sensor data is noisy enough to trigger false alarms. Rather than tune the threshold to compensate, the decision was to add a multi-sample noise-filtering stage before threshold detection — trading a small amount of latency for alerts people can actually trust.

Signal chain: sensor → noise filter → threshold detection → GSM → SMS to farmers

The Build

A validated diversion channel, not just an alert.

  • Water-level sensor with multi-sample filtering and microcontroller threshold detection
  • Automated GSM SMS alert triggered on threshold breach
  • Diversion channel and retention pit sized with hydraulic calculations for slope and cross-section
  • Scale hydraulic model pour tests to confirm channel geometry before real-world construction

Diversion channel and retention pit, geometry confirmed with scale hydraulic pour tests

The Result

Presented to city council. Selected for construction.

The system and channel design were presented to Kigali City Council and to more than 30 schools at Science Day 2024, and the design was selected for community construction.
