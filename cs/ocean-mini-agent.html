Ocean Mini Agent

An end-to-end SME trade finance underwriting engine bridging cash-upfront suppliers and credit-seeking buyers, with automated payment execution.

2
Sequential decision gates, each with quantitative thresholds
The Constraint

Underwriting has to be fast, auditable, and defensible.

Extending credit to SMEs requires a decision process that's consistent, explainable, and fast enough to not stall a trade — while still being rigorous enough to bound risk on both the supplier and buyer side.

The Decision

Two sequential gates, each a hard quantitative filter.

Rather than a single blended risk score, the logic is split into two sequential gates — eligibility, then financial health — each with explicit numeric thresholds, so every approval, rejection, or request-for-info comes with a traceable reason.

Two-gate underwriting decision tree — each branch returns a structured outcome with reasoning

The Build

Credit sizing formulas, and a live payment integration behind them.

Credit limit = min(10 × 90-day ADB adjusted for volatility, 20% × annual revenue)
Repayment = Total limit / 16 weeks · weekly direct debit
  • Three structured outcomes — approve / reject / request-info — each returned with explicit reasoning
  • Flask backend acting as a secure credential bridge
  • n8n workflow engine for auditable branching and full execution history
  • Live GoCardless sandbox integration for direct debit payment automation

Frontend → Flask (credential bridge) → n8n (auditable logic) → GoCardless sandbox

The Result

Every decision is explainable and payment is automated end to end.

The engine produces one of three structured outcomes for every application, each with a returned reason, and executes approved repayment schedules automatically through a live GoCardless sandbox integration — underwriting logic that's fast, consistent, and auditable.
